More and more companies are investing in charging infrastructure. In practice, many of those charging sites are still not used to their full potential, and that is exactly where the opportunity lies.

That is why ChargeNet is proud to announce its partnership with Equans E-Mobility. Equans builds and manages charging sites for customers across the Netherlands, and together with ChargeNet, that infrastructure can now be opened up selectively to third parties, while owners keep full control over who charges there, when, and under what conditions.

What This Partnership Delivers

Together, ChargeNet and Equans E-Mobility create a network where:

  • Charging capacity is used more efficiently, instead of sitting idle outside a site`s own operating hours,
  • Extra revenue is generated from infrastructure that is already in place,
  • Electric transport scales more easily, as more charging locations become accessible to more transport companies,
  • The business case for charging infrastructure gets stronger, with a shorter payback period for site owners

Full Control, Selective Access

As with every ChargeNet partnership, control stays firmly with the site owner. Equans customers who join the ChargeNet network decide exactly which companies get access, at what times, and under what conditions charging takes place.
