Making a fleet of trucks or vans more sustainable is a big step, and one of the first questions that comes up is simple: where and when will I actually be able to charge? Equans E-mobility knows that hesitation well, and their story shows what happens once a charging site starts getting discovered by the right drivers.

From Own Fleet to Full Charging Site

Equans E-mobility opened its first charging site three years ago and has since added several more locations right along the highway. Over time, the team noticed something interesting: longer charging sessions were showing up more and more often at their fast chargers. Someone besides their own fleet was clearly charging there. As it turned out, e-trucks had started finding the site on their own.

That discovery led to one clear next step: make the site known to transport companies on purpose, rather than leaving it to chance. That is how Equans E-mobility connected with ChargeNet, the platform that helps transport companies find and share charging infrastructure.

Now Live: Equans E-mobility`s Nieuwegein Location

Through ChargeNet, Equans E-mobility`s charging sites are now easy for transport companies to find, with a competitive charging rate and the ability to start a session directly through the ChargeNet app. The site at Arsenaaldok 9, Nieuwegein, is now live on the ChargeNet network and ready to welcome e-trucks.

Ready to Charge There?

If you are planning routes near Nieuwegein and want a reliable, competitively priced place to charge your e-truck, Equans E-mobility`s location is now part of the ChargeNet network. Get in touch with ChargeNet to find this and other charging sites along your routes.
