ChargeNet @ Move East: Shaping the Future of Sustainable Mobility

On October 11, the second edition of Move East took place at Industriepark Kleefse Waard (IPKW) in Arnhem, the leading event in the east of the Netherlands focused on sustainable business mobility. This year’s theme, “The Netherlands in 2030,” looked ahead to a decade of transformation in how we move, work, and power our economy.

The Netherlands in 2030

The program explored key challenges and opportunities on the road to zero-emission mobility, from charging infrastructure under grid congestion to alternative fuels and emission-free entrepreneurship. Experts, policymakers, and innovators came together to discuss how the mobility sector can adapt to meet national climate goals.

E-Mobility for the Future

Move East is a collaboration hub where business leaders and technology providers explore practical solutions for the energy transition. With e-mobility developing rapidly, the event provided valuable insights into how companies can prepare for an electric future and what opportunities it brings for logistics, transport, and energy management.

ChargeNet’s Contribution

As an innovator in electric truck charging, ChargeNet joined Move East to share our vision for smarter, more connected charging infrastructure. Our platform helps logistics companies and fleet operators find, access, and manage charging locations efficiently, even under the pressure of grid limitations. By connecting businesses, infrastructure owners, and energy providers, we’re helping build the foundation for the sustainable transport ecosystem envisioned for 2030.

A Step Forward for Clean Mobility

Events like Move East play a vital role in accelerating the mobility transition, by connecting ideas, people, and technologies that make zero-emission logistics possible. ChargeNet is proud to be part of this movement, working together to make sustainable business mobility a reality.
