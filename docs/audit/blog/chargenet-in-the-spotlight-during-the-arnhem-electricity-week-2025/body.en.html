ChargeNet at Arnhem Electricity Week 2025: Showcasing ChargeBase together with Volta Energy & AVIA VOLT

From May 19–22, 2025, ChargeNet is part of Arnhem Electricity Week: a major annual event that brings together innovators, entrepreneurs, policymakers, and researchers to explore the future of clean energy and electrification.

This year, ChargeNet joins forces with Volta Energy and AVIA VOLT NL at the Euro Demo Days in Arnhem to showcase ChargeBase; a groundbreaking mobile charging solution that enables clean, off-grid charging anywhere.

ChargeBase combines solar power, hydrogen battery storage, and mobile charging infrastructure to provide flexible and sustainable charging in places without access to the power grid, such as construction sites, events, and logistics hubs. Together, Volta Energy, AVIA VOLT, and ChargeNet demonstrate how renewable energy and smart software can accelerate the transition toward a zero-emission future.

Visitors of Arnhem Electricity Week are welcome to visit our stand at the Euro Demo Days to see ChargeBase in action, learn about our collaboration, and discover how we’re making charging accessible wherever it’s needed.
