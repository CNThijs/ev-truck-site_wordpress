ChargeNet, Volta Energy & AVIA VOLT Join Forces

From May 19–22, 2025, ChargeNet, Volta Energy Nederland, and AVIA VOLT NL are teaming up at the Euro Demo Days in Arnhem to present ChargeBase; a groundbreaking mobile charging solution that enables clean, off-grid charging anywhere.

Clean power, anywhere

ChargeBase demonstrates how renewable energy and smart technology can make charging possible even in locations without a fixed grid connection, such as construction sites, festivals, or temporary logistics hubs.

How ChargeBase works:

PartnerRole
Volta Energyharvests solar power on a remote location and temporarily stores the generated electricity in its innovative hydrogen battery system.
AVIA VOLTacting as Charge Point Operator (CPO), provides and manages the mobile charger that delivers this power to electric vehicles.
ChargeNetconnects everything digitally; offering access control for authorized users, enabling them to start and stop charging sessions, while managing all administrative and financial transactions in the background.

This integrated setup shows how clean energy and digital innovation can come together to create a flexible, grid-independent charging infrastructure, eliminating the need for diesel generators and reducing CO₂ emissions.

Visitors to the Euro Demo Days during Arnhem Electricity Week can see the solution live at the Demo Field, where ChargeBase will showcase the future of mobile, sustainable charging.

🌱 Smart, mobile, and sustainable; ChargeBase is powering the transition to clean mobility.
