The energy transition calls for smart, practical solutions: especially in sectors like construction, where logistics and sustainability must go hand in hand. That’s why ChargeNet, together with Volta Energy and AVIA Volt, has developed ChargeBase: a mobile, solar-powered charging solution designed specifically for construction sites.

From Idea to Demonstration

The development of ChargeBase was made possible by the Demonstration Energy Innovation Voucher from Oost NL’s Groeiversneller program. This support allowed ChargeNet to move from concept to demonstration model and to showcase the solution during Arnhem Electricity Week, where it received enthusiastic feedback from potential users.

An Extension of the ChargeNet Platform

ChargeBase is not a stand-alone product; it is an expansion of the existing ChargeNet platform. Transport and logistics companies with a ChargeNet account can seamlessly use ChargeBase alongside other charging services, making it a natural fit within our ecosystem of affordable, accessible, and smart charging solutions.

Powered by Collaboration

The project highlights the crucial role of collaboration within the Connectr and Oost NL networks. Oost NL not only provided funding but also introduced ChargeNet to Volta Energy and continues to offer mentorship and guidance.

Looking Ahead

With ChargeBase, ChargeNet is making charging infrastructure more flexible, sustainable, and relevant—bringing reliable, mobile power directly to construction sites. It’s a small push with big impact, helping accelerate the path to zero-emission construction logistics.
