THE Hub for Collaboration and Clean Energy Innovation

We’re excited to announce that ChargeNet has moved into its new office space at the Connectr Innovation Lab on Cleantech Park Arnhem!

The Connectr Innovation Lab is the beating heart of the Gelderland energy cluster; a place where startups, established companies, knowledge institutions, and governments come together to collaborate and accelerate the energy transition.

Located within the Connectr Shared Office, ChargeNet now shares its workspace with other innovative organizations that are shaping the future of clean mobility and sustainable energy. With over 100 flexible workstations, shared facilities, and a thriving network of professionals, this environment fosters connection, knowledge exchange, and co-creation.

For ChargeNet, being part of the Connectr ecosystem means more than just a new office. It’s an opportunity to work side-by-side with partners, gain access to cutting-edge research, and stay closely connected to developments in electric mobility and smart charging infrastructure.

Together, we’re driving the transition toward a zero-emission logistics sector, right from the heart of the Dutch clean energy innovation hub.
