> Partnership
> Our network now includes affordable access to LEAP24`s public e-truck charging sites, giving transport companies more flexibility on the road.
> Electrifying transport takes more than infrastructure on your own site. It also takes reliable charging on the road. ChargeNet`s semi-public network of private charging locations now extends further, with discounted access to LEAP24`s public e-truck charging sites.
> One Network, More Places to Charge
> Through this partnership, ChargeNet users get access to a charging network built specifically for e-trucks, at rates significantly lower than standard public charging cards. In practice, that means:
> More flexibility for electric trucks on the road, beyond your own charging site,
> Lower charging costs while en route, thanks to preferential LEAP24 rates,
> One charging card for both fixed spots at transport locations and public top-up charging.
> Why This Matters for Route Planning
> Private charging sites solve a large part of the electrification puzzle, but trucks still need dependable places to top up between fixed locations. By connecting to LEAP24`s public charging network, ChargeNet closes that gap. Transport companies can now plan routes with confidence, knowing that both private and public charging options are available through a single platform and a single card.
> Strengthening the ChargeNet Network
> This partnership makes the ChargeNet network more complete and, as a result, makes electric transport easier to scale and more profitable to operate. Together, ChargeNet and LEAP24 are building a more efficient and more accessible charging network for the logistics sector.
> ChargeNet: the charging card for logistics and construction. Get in touch to find out how the LEAP24 integration can support your routes.
