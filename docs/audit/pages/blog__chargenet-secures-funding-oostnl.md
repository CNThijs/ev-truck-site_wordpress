> Back to Blog
> ChargeNet Secures Funding to Accelerate Smart Charging for Electric Trucks
> May 20, 2025
> ChargeNet
> Related article
> Accelerate
> ChargeNet has received early-stage funding from Oost NL’s Vroegefasefonds Gelderland.
> Vroegefasefonds Gelderland
> We’re excited to announce that ChargeNet has received early-stage funding from Oost NL’s Vroegefasefonds Gelderland. This investment supports the development of our platform that connects logistics companies with private charging points, making it easier to charge electric trucks along their routes.
> With electric truck adoption growing and public charging infrastructure lagging behind, ChargeNet unlocks existing, underused chargers at warehouses, retailers, and logistics hubs. Our platform offers real-time availability, pricing, booking, and automated invoicing—simplifying charging for both fleet owners and infrastructure providers.
> What the Funding Enables
> Build out a Minimum Viable Product (MVP) app and platform for both infrastructure owners and truck drivers
> Pilot demonstration projects with retailers and transport companies to validate and refine the model
> Expand features such as automated charging, usage tracking, dynamic pricing, and admin/invoicing tools
> Professionalize operations and establish strong local roots in Arnhem’s Cleantech Park
> Strategic re-location to Cleantech Park Arnhem
> ChargeNet has relocated to the Cleantech Park Arnhem (formerly IPKW), a hub for clean energy innovators. This location places the team in close proximity to Connectr and a network of partners, fostering collaboration and knowledge sharing. Oost NL helped facilitate ChargeNet’s “soft landing” in the region, further strengthening their position.
> This funding and our re-location will help us develop our MVP, run pilot projects, and expand from our new base at Cleantech Park Arnhem. Together, we’re building the foundation for a cleaner, more efficient transport future.
> Keep charging ahead!
