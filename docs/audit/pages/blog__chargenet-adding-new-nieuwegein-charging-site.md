> Equans E-mobility opened its first charging site three years ago and has since added several more locations right along the highway. Over time, the team noticed something interesting: longer charging sessions were showing up more and more often at their fast chargers. Someone besides their own fleet was clearly charging there. As it turned out, e-trucks had started finding the site on their own.
> That discovery led to one clear next step: make the site known to transport companies on purpose, rather than leaving it to chance. That is how Equans E-mobility connected with ChargeNet, the platform that helps transport companies find and share charging infrastructure.
