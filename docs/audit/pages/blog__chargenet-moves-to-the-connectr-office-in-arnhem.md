> For ChargeNet, being part of the Connectr ecosystem means more than just a new office. It’s an opportunity to work side-by-side with partners, gain access to cutting-edge research, and stay closely connected to developments in electric mobility and smart charging infrastructure.
> Together, we’re driving the transition toward a zero-emission logistics sector, right from the heart of the Dutch clean energy innovation hub.
